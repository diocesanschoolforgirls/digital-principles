Our stance

Our stance

a stance, not a strategy.

Technology is no longer a separate domain that needs its own governance silo. It's infrastructure and medium, the water everything swims in. Rules date quickly, especially in fast-moving areas like AI, where prescription becomes obsolete before the ink dries. An effective approach that meets our needs is a set of core principles that set boundaries and articulate values, leaving implementation to professional judgement. The principles flex as the landscape changes, but they always remain grounded in our values of Honesty, Integrity, Respectfulness and Empathy.
